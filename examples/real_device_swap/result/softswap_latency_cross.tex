%% Same-PHY rows alternate the A and B flows of one PHY. "(cross)" rows come
%% from the alternating 802.15.4 / 802.11ah sweep, split by the flow the swap

%% more than arriving from another 802.15.4 flow.
%%
%% These are ~1.3x lower than the idle back-to-back softswap_latency figures:
%% there, 50 ms of settle between swaps lets the runtime's worker threads
%% deschedule, so each swap pays a cold start that a busy receiver does not.
